# Personas — DisruptionAI

## Persona 1: Mr. John — Production Planner

### Demographics
- **Name**: Mr. John
- **Role**: Senior Production Planner, ABC Steel Corporation
- **Experience**: 12 years in steel manufacturing production planning
- **Location**: Columbus, OH (headquarters)
- **Reports To**: VP of Operations

### Primary Goal
Minimize customer impact and maximize order fulfillment when a catastrophic disruption forces cross-plant production reallocation.

### Key Frustrations (Current Manual Process)
- Spends 2-3 days manually cross-referencing spreadsheets to assess disruption impact
- Cannot simultaneously evaluate equipment compatibility, material availability, machine utilization, and cascade impact — has to check each sequentially
- Misses cascade impacts where transferring one order pushes another past its deadline
- No easy way to run what-if scenarios without rebuilding spreadsheets from scratch
- Customer communications are delayed because the reallocation plan takes too long to finalize

### Decision-Making Style
- Data-driven but relies on experience for edge cases
- Prefers to see all constraints laid out before making a decision
- Wants to understand the "why" behind every recommendation — doesn't trust black-box outputs
- Comfortable overriding AI recommendations when business context demands it
- Iterative: likes to evaluate options one at a time rather than seeing a complete plan upfront

### Technical Comfort
- Proficient with ERP systems and spreadsheets
- Comfortable with chat-based interfaces (uses Teams/Slack daily)
- Not a developer — needs clear, non-technical explanations
- Expects structured data (tables, summaries) alongside conversational text

### Time Pressure Context
- During a disruption, every hour of delay means potential penalty clauses and customer churn
- Needs to go from "plant down" to "reallocation plan approved" in hours, not days
- Must communicate with customers within 24 hours of disruption
